Crew Up — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Crew Up, formerly RideOS, helps motorcycle and car enthusiasts record rides and connect with other users. This notice describes the account, location and social information used by the app. Contact the developer at julian3390@gmail.com about privacy, content reports, or deletion requests.

Accounts and profiles

Accounts use an identifier and can have an email address, password, handle, display name, avatar, biography and date of birth. Supabase provides authentication and backend storage. Email addresses are used for account access and recovery, not displayed as public profile information. Session credentials remain on your device so you can stay signed in.

The app uses the supplied date of birth for age-related controls, including its restriction on public map publishing for under-18 users. This is not independent age verification. The existing public-map age check runs in the app; this notice does not claim that every backend request independently verifies age.

Ride information on your device

The local database holds ride recordings and available sensor observations, route history, vehicle details, fuel entries and preferences. Ride recording does not depend on continuous internet access. A local ride recording is separate from information you choose to publish or share. Uninstalling can remove local information; it does not delete your cloud account or content already shared with others.

Location and sharing

Location is used for recording, map centering, weather and optional sharing. An active ride can request background location, including while the screen is locked. Permissions and operating-system behavior affect recording; background operation is not guaranteed after the app is forcibly closed.

Group sharing sends position and selected signals to the backend under the session's sharing controls and expiry. Check the app's audience and freshness indicators. Some assistance signals ask to share a position explicitly. An arrival confirmation should not be interpreted as a published home address.

Open World has separate choices for sharing with mutual followers and showing an approximate public vehicle marker. Both are off until enabled. Mutual sharing can reveal identity and a shared position to eligible mutuals. Public presence uses a roughly 400-metre grid rather than an exact position and does not display the account identity. Approximate location is not a guarantee of anonymity.

A ride is not required for foreground Open World sharing. Outside an active ride, leaving the foreground stops regular presence updates. A previous position may remain until expiry or removal succeeds; timestamps distinguish recent information from stale information. Clusters are presentations of authorized presence, not proof that everyone is attending a meet or standing at a specific address.

Posts, stories and messages

Content you submit, including text, photos, videos and conversation content, is stored in the backend so the intended app audience can access it. Public social posts should not be treated as private messages. Audience rules differ between features. Blocking can restrict app visibility and contact, but cannot recall downloaded content or erase messages already received.

Stories normally stop appearing to other users after 24 hours. Expiry is not deletion: rows, retained media files and downloaded copies have different lifetimes. The author may retain backend access to their own files. View lists are intended for the story author; likes are available to the author and the person who left the like.

New supported story-photo uploads remove JPEG location metadata; text added in the editor is drawn into the uploaded picture. This does not remove location visible in the picture itself or establish that historical files were reprocessed.

Story images use expiring signed links rather than public links. New links are subject to backend visibility rules. The app normally requests links lasting up to 15 minutes and no longer than the story's remaining lifetime. This is the app's requested lifetime, not a server-enforced maximum for every possible caller. A previously issued link can remain usable until its own expiry. Downloads, screenshots and content previously obtained through public links cannot be recalled.

Messages and attachments are intended for conversation participants. No claim of end-to-end encryption is made. Some other social media files can have public URLs; do not assume every media feature has the same private-link protection as Stories.

Content checks, reports and blocking

Crew Up checks specified submitted story/post text for a limited set of explicit threats, hateful harassment and sexual-exploitation phrases. A refusal should explain the problem and keep the draft available to edit. This is limited text screening, not general language understanding or photo-pixel classification. No photo is sent to a new moderation vendor, no manual prepublication approval queue is introduced, and no account penalty is imposed by these checks.

Use the app's report and block controls for abusive content or contact. Reports are stored for developer review. Reporting a story hides it from the reporter; some other reported content can be hidden pending review. Contact the developer using the email above for concerns. This notice does not promise an unmeasured response deadline.

Service providers and permissions

  • Supabase handles accounts, shared records, media storage and backend services. Operational services may process request and security metadata.
  • Mapbox, Google Maps or Apple Maps provide maps according to the selected provider and screen. Their requests reveal the viewed area and ordinary network metadata to that provider.
  • Weather requests use Open-Meteo with a rounded location. Providers also receive ordinary network information such as an IP address.
  • Permission-based notifications use a device token. Photos, camera, microphone and location permissions enable their corresponding features; manage permissions in device settings.

Retention and deletion

  • Local recordings remain on the device until removed or local app data is deleted.
  • Shared presence is subject to expiry; a temporary failure can delay deletion or removal from another device's display.
  • Story rows are configured for later cleanup after visibility expires. Picture files can remain stored, including orphaned uploads; there is currently no general automated orphan-file collector.
  • Cloud profiles, messages, reports and other social content have no general automatic deletion period established for this release. They can remain stored until an applicable deletion or retention action is completed. Deleting an app is not a cloud-data deletion request.
  • Use the in-app Delete my account action for account deletion, or contact the developer about an account or data request. Backups, security obligations and copies held by other users can limit immediate removal; do not assume that disappearing from a screen means every copy is erased.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice when the app's data handling changes. Check the published date. Questions, deletion requests and concerns can be sent to julian3390@gmail.com.
