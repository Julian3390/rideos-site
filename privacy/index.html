RideOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

RideOS is a riding companion for motorcyclists. It records rides using the phone's own sensors and, when you choose to, lets you share your position with a group of riders. This policy describes exactly what it collects, where that goes, and what it never does.

No account, and no identity we can read

RideOS asks for no email address, no name, no phone number and no password. Group riding uses an anonymous identifier issued by our backend so that riders in the same group can be told apart. It is a random value. It is not linked to you, and we cannot use it to find out who you are.

What stays on your phone

Everything RideOS records is stored in a database on your device:

  • GPS position, speed, heading and altitude, recorded while a ride is running
  • Accelerometer and gyroscope readings taken alongside those positions
  • Your ride history, motorcycles, fuel entries and settings

None of this is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone, and when

Weather

To show conditions and hazard warnings, RideOS sends a location to Open‑Meteo (api.open-meteo.com and air-quality-api.open-meteo.com). That location is deliberately rounded to roughly five kilometres before it is sent, because weather does not change over a few streets and a precise position is not needed to look it up. No identifier is attached to the request.

Maps

Map tiles are fetched from Mapbox (api.mapbox.com) or drawn by Apple Maps, depending on which you have selected. Fetching a tile tells that provider roughly which area is being displayed. Their handling of those requests is governed by their own privacy policies.

Group riding

Nothing about a group ride leaves your phone unless you join a group and switch sharing on. When you do:

  • Your position is sent to our backend so other riders in that group can see it
  • Sharing is always for a set time that you choose — 30 minutes, 2 hours or 6 hours. There is no option to share indefinitely.
  • When that time runs out your position is erased from the server, not merely hidden from the display
  • Only riders in the same group can read it. This is enforced by the database itself, not by the app's screens.
  • Quick signals you send ("need gas", "running late") are visible to that group

The signal "I'm home" never carries a location, by design, so that telling your group you have arrived does not disclose where you live.

What RideOS does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services. There are no third-party tracking SDKs in the app at all.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites
  • No background location collection when you are not recording a ride or sharing with a group

Location permission

RideOS needs location access to do the one thing it exists for: record where and how you rode. If you grant "While Using", recording continues with the screen locked but can be interrupted by the system. "Always" makes long rides more reliable. You can revoke either at any time in iOS Settings; the app will keep working and simply stop recording.

Deleting your data

Ride data lives on your phone — delete a ride in the app, or delete the app, and it is gone. Anything shared with a group expires and is erased automatically; leaving a group removes your row from it immediately.

Children

RideOS is intended for licensed motorcycle riders and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published here before the change takes effect.
